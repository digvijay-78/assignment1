# THE DAY THE SCHOOL LOST ITS MEMORY

## SET 1 — strangely | normally | confusedly | hurriedly | anxiously

It was an ordinary Monday morning until something **strangely** happened at Greenfield School. Students entered the building **normally**, chatting about their weekend. However, several teachers were standing **confusedly** in the corridor. The principal walked **hurriedly** towards the staff room and looked around **anxiously**.

## SET 2 — curiously | immediately | blankly | completely | oddly

“What is happening?” Maya asked **curiously** as she entered her classroom. Nobody answered her **immediately**. Her English teacher was staring **blankly** at the blackboard. She seemed to have **completely** forgotten what lesson she was supposed to teach. Even more **oddly**, the students could not remember their own timetable.

## SET 3 — suspiciously | quietly | slowly | desperately | eventually

Maya looked at her friend Kabir **suspiciously**. “Do you remember what period we have now?” she whispered **quietly**. Kabir shook his head **slowly**. They searched their notebooks **desperately**, but there was no timetable anywhere. **Eventually**, Maya noticed something even stranger.

## SET 4 — simultaneously | occasionally | carefully | gently | suddenly

Every clock in the classroom had stopped **suddenly** at exactly 9:17. The second hand on the wall clock was moving **occasionally**, but it never completed a full circle. Kabir examined it **carefully** and tapped the glass **gently**. **Simultaneously**, the bell rang.

## SET 5 — unexpectedly | strangely | aimlessly | hesitantly | collectively

The bell sounded **unexpectedly**, and everyone rushed into the corridor. **Aimlessly**, nobody seemed to know where to go. Students wandered **hesitantly** through the hallways while teachers gave instructions **collectively**. Maya realized that the entire school had somehow forgotten its daily routine **strangely**.

## SET 6 — independently | politely | slowly | perplexedly | seriously

Maya and Kabir decided to investigate the situation **independently**. They first went to the principal's office and knocked **politely**. The principal opened the door **slowly** and looked at them **perplexedly**. “Do I know you?” he asked **seriously**.

## SET 7 — astonishedly | patiently | deeply | frantically | unfortunately

The two friends stared at him **astonishedly**. “Sir, we've been in your school for three years,” Kabir replied **patiently**. The principal frowned **deeply** and searched through a pile of files **frantically**. **Unfortunately**, none of the student records contained their names.

## SET 8 — increasingly | immediately | reluctantly | perfectly | remarkably

Maya became **increasingly** worried. She suggested checking the school's security cameras **immediately**. The principal agreed **reluctantly** and followed them to the control room. The cameras were functioning **perfectly**, but the recordings showed something **remarkably** unusual.

## SET 9 — closely | briefly | curiously | extremely

At exactly 9:17 that morning, every person in the school had stopped moving for three seconds. Then everyone continued walking as if nothing had happened. Maya watched the recording **curiously**. She noticed a faint flash appearing **briefly** on every screen. **Closely**, the flash seemed to originate from the science laboratory. Kabir immediately became **extremely** interested.

## SET 10 — mysteriously | cautiously | perfectly | neatly | systematically

The science laboratory was normally kept locked during lunch breaks, but that day the door was standing **mysteriously** open. Maya pushed it **cautiously**. Inside, everything appeared **perfectly** ordinary. Beakers stood **neatly** on the shelves, and the microscopes were arranged **systematically** on the tables.

## SET 11 — rhythmically | hesitantly | carefully | suddenly | instinctively

Then Kabir noticed a small machine beneath the teacher's desk. It was blinking **rhythmically**. Maya approached it **hesitantly** and examined its controls **carefully**. A tiny screen displayed the number 9:17. **Suddenly**, the machine began making a low humming sound. They stepped backwards **instinctively**.

## SET 12 — abruptly | cautiously | silently | obviously

The machine switched itself off **abruptly**. A piece of paper emerged from a narrow slot. Maya picked it up **cautiously**. It contained only one sentence: “Memory has been temporarily suspended.” Kabir read it **silently** twice. **Obviously**, someone had deliberately created the machine.

## SET 13 — thoroughly | carefully | interestingly | thoughtfully

They decided to search the laboratory **thoroughly**. Behind a cupboard, Maya discovered a hidden drawer. She opened it **carefully** and found several photographs. They showed the school from different angles. **Interestingly**, every photograph had been taken at exactly 9:17. Kabir studied them **thoughtfully**.

## SET 14 — partially | slowly | surprisingly | nervously

One photograph showed a person standing outside the school gate. The face was **partially** hidden by a hood. Maya zoomed in **slowly** on the photograph. **Surprisingly**, she recognized the person's jacket. It belonged to someone who worked at the school. Kabir looked at Maya **nervously**.

## SET 15 — quietly | secretly | unusually | desperately

They hurried **quietly** back towards the main building. Instead of going directly to the principal, they decided to observe the staff **secretly**. Teachers were behaving **unusually**. Some were staring at old photographs, while others were **desperately** trying to remember important events. The school seemed to be slowly losing its past.

## SET 16 — rapidly | silently | carefully | anxiously | cautiously

Maya noticed the librarian walking **anxiously** towards the archive room. She followed her **silently** and watched as the librarian unlocked a cabinet. Inside were hundreds of old school records. The librarian removed one file **cautiously** and examined it **carefully**. Maya stepped forward **rapidly**.

## SET 17 — politely | abruptly | faintly | quietly | slowly

“Excuse me,” Maya said **quietly**. The librarian turned around **slowly**. For several seconds, neither spoke. Then the librarian smiled **politely**. “You remember, don't you?” she asked **faintly**. Maya nodded **abruptly**.

## SET 18 — artificially | unfortunately | accidentally | rapidly

The librarian explained that the machine had been designed years ago by a brilliant science teacher. He believed that human memories could be temporarily erased **artificially**. The machine had never been tested on an entire school before. **Unfortunately**, someone had activated it **accidentally** that morning. The consequences were spreading **rapidly**.

## SET 19 — seriously | attentively | immediately | quietly | instantly

“If we don't stop it, everyone may forget everything,” the librarian warned **seriously**. Maya and Kabir listened **attentively**. They needed to find the machine's creator **immediately**. The librarian pointed towards an old photograph hanging **quietly** above the archive door. Maya recognized the face **instantly**.

## SET 20 — surprisingly | hurriedly | briskly | fortunately

The man in the photograph was Professor Arvind Rao, who had retired from the school fifteen years earlier. **Surprisingly**, the librarian revealed that he still lived nearby. Maya and Kabir left the school **hurriedly**. They walked **briskly** through the streets while discussing their plan. **Fortunately**, the professor's house was only ten minutes away.

## SET 21 — firmly | cautiously | curiously | immediately | quietly

They knocked on the door **firmly**. After a few moments, an elderly man opened it **cautiously**. He looked at the students **curiously**. When Maya mentioned the school, his expression changed **immediately**. He invited them inside **quietly**.

## SET 22 — carefully | thoughtfully | deeply | directly

The professor listened **carefully** as Maya explained what had happened. He remained silent **thoughtfully** for several seconds. Then he sighed **deeply**. “I warned them never to activate that machine,” he said **directly**. Kabir asked **directly** who had switched it on.

## SET 23 — seriously | honestly | slowly | carefully

The professor looked at them **seriously**. “I don't know,” he admitted **honestly**. “But there is one way to reverse the effect.” He walked **slowly** towards a locked cabinet. From inside, he removed a small metal key. He held it **carefully** in his hand.

## SET 24 — eagerly | immediately | cautiously | rhythmically

The key activated a hidden compartment beneath the machine. According to the professor, the compartment contained a control device that could restore everyone's memories. Maya listened **eagerly**. They returned to school **immediately** and entered the laboratory **cautiously**. The machine was still blinking **rhythmically**.

## SET 25 — deliberately | initially | violently | rapidly | instinctively

The professor inserted the control device **deliberately**. Nothing happened **initially**. Then the machine began vibrating **violently**. Lights flashed **rapidly** across the room. Everyone stepped backwards **instinctively**.

## SET 26 — frantically | gradually | relievedly | slowly

For several seconds, the laboratory remained completely silent. Then the principal rushed into the room **frantically**. He suddenly remembered everything. Teachers began remembering their students, and students remembered their classes **gradually**. Maya looked around **relievedly**. The school was returning to normal **slowly**.

## SET 27 — sincerely | proudly | wisely | carefully

The principal thanked Maya and Kabir **sincerely**. He admitted that without their curiosity, nobody might have discovered the cause. The professor smiled **proudly**. “Never underestimate the power of observation,” he said **wisely**. Maya remembered his words **carefully**.

## SET 28 — cheerfully | confidently | punctually | mysteriously

By the end of the day, everything seemed normal again. Students walked through the corridors **cheerfully**, teachers resumed their lessons **confidently**, and the school bell rang **punctually**. Yet Maya noticed one strange thing. The clock in the laboratory still showed 9:17 **mysteriously**.

## SET 29 — quietly | carefully | surprisingly | deliberately

That evening, Maya returned home **quietly**. She opened her notebook and wrote down everything that had happened **carefully**. She wanted to make sure she would never forget the strange day. **Surprisingly**, however, one page of her notebook was already filled with handwriting she did not recognize. The final sentence had been written **deliberately**.

## SET 30 — slowly | silently | carefully | firmly | mysteriously

Maya read the sentence **slowly**: “The machine was not activated by accident.” She stared at the words **silently**. Someone had planned the entire incident **deliberately**. Maya closed the notebook **firmly** and smiled **mysteriously**. She knew that the real investigation had only just begun.
