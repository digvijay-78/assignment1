Question-23
Which statement is TRUE?

A. If a tuple contains a mutable object, the tuple itself becomes mutable.
B. A tuple guarantees that the complete object graph reachable through its elements is immutable.
C. A tuple prevents modification of its own element references, but it does not necessarily prevent mutation of objects referenced by those elements.

Question-24
